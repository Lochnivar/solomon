# Glossary — Canonical Term Definitions

Reference document for consistent usage across all project files. When a term appears in any file, it should conform to the definitions here. When a new term needs establishing, add it here first.

---

## Cosmological Entities

### The Fallen
Former divine beings — members of the original heavenly court who explicitly rebelled against the divine order. The oldest and most powerful supernatural entities in the setting. Not a unified force: they operate more like a cartel, with shared interests, significant internal conflict, and ancient grievances among themselves that predate their opposition to Solomon's organization by millennia.

Approximately a dozen significant Fallen remain active. They are patient, strategic, and operate on timescales of centuries. They are the **source** of whatever the sulfur threshold chemistry is actually measuring — not subject to it in the way Nephilim-descended lineages are.

They gave rise to the Nephilim through unions with human women. They regard their offspring's remnant spirits (demons) with contempt — those spirits are their nature expressed without restraint, which they find both embarrassing and operationally inconvenient.

**What the Fallen are NOT:** They are not demons. They are not Nephilim. They are not dead. They are the original adversaries — ancient, embodied (in some form), and still operational.

Key named Fallen: Ashtoreth (corruption and infiltration — primary architect of Solomon's original fall), Moloch (sacrifice and suffering as power), Chemosh (war and nationalist identity weaponized).

---

### Nephilim
The offspring of Fallen and human women. Half divine heritage, half human biology. The divine inheritance passed through human biology and came out transformed — more volatile, more appetite-driven, less patient, less controlled than the Fallen themselves. The human half did not simply dilute the Fallen nature; it destabilized it.

**The original Nephilim are dead.** They were killed in the flood. When they died, their spirits did not exit through the normal human pathway — the divine inheritance prevented it. Those spirits persisted as demons. See: Demons.

Their biological condition, however, persisted and reproduced independently across millennia. The lineages they seeded are still alive, still active, and still carry the Nephilim heritage in their biology.

**"Nephilim" in operational usage** refers to any living being carrying that inherited biological heritage — vampires, lycanthropes, cryptid lineages, and the broader spectrum of beings descended from the original generation. This is technically imprecise (the actual Nephilim are dead) but is standard shorthand within the organization. Everyone understands it to mean "Nephilim-descended."

The oldest surviving lineages have their own cultures, histories, and political structures entirely separate from Solomon's world. The Fae traditions across global folklore were observing these oldest lineages from the outside.

**Dormant carriers** are individuals with Nephilim heritage that has not manifested — they appear human, may have no knowledge of their lineage, and register above baseline but below first threshold on screening.

**What Nephilim are NOT:** The original generation are not alive. Living "Nephilim" are descendants, not the originals. They are not Fallen. They are not demons. They are not simply "monsters" — the category includes a wide range of entities, some predatory, some not, with their own internal diversity.

---

### Demons
The disembodied spirits of dead Nephilim. Per the 1 Enoch tradition: when a Nephilim dies, the divine inheritance in their nature prevents normal exit from physical existence. The spirit persists in a disembodied state, stripped of the physical form it was shaped for, driven by appetites it can no longer satisfy directly.

Demons are obsessed with possession because possession is the closest available approximation of the embodied existence they lost. They are not using a host as a tool — they are trying to re-establish the physical-metaphysical integration their own biology once provided.

The Fallen are the demons' grandparents in a cosmological sense — Fallen nature, passed through Nephilim biology, persists in the demon consciousness after the biology fails. This is why Levi's threshold-based binding operations work on entities with no biological substrate: the operations are working at the level of the inherited nature, not the chemistry.

Demons view the Fallen as stodgy, over-organized, and too cautious to act effectively. The Fallen view demons as embarrassing evidence of what their nature looks like without discipline. The contempt is mutual. The fracture is operationally exploitable.

**What demons are NOT:** They are not the Fallen. They are not living Nephilim. They are not generic evil spirits of non-Nephilim origin — that category may or may not exist and is a separate question. In this setting, "demon" has a specific cosmological meaning: disembodied dead Nephilim.

---

### Monster Lineages
Nephilim-descended lineages whose biology has crossed into clearly supernatural territory and reproduces independently. Vampires and lycanthropes are the primary examples. Others exist.

Monster lineages are living beings (with the exception of vampires, who are dead in the biological sense but metaphysically sustained). They are not demons. They are not Fallen. They are the living end of the Nephilim heritage — lineages that have been shaped by centuries or millennia of independent development into distinct species with their own biology, culture, and internal politics.

Some are predatory. Some are not. Gad assesses case by case. The existence of monster lineages that are neutral or allied is a standing feature of the setting, not an exception.

---

### The Repentant
A small, scattered faction of Fallen who have turned away from the Anti-Council's agenda. They are **not redeemed** — the rebellion cannot be uncrossed. They are in an ongoing process of reformation that is genuine, incomplete, and subject to failure.

The Repentant are hunted by the Anti-Council as traitors and as examples. They are never fully trusted by Solomon's organization. They occupy an uncomfortable position in every relationship they have.

Solomon extends his good word with the divine on their behalf — a costly gesture given that he is not certain his word carries weight in that direction. He does it because the alternative is deciding their effort means nothing, and he cannot make that judgment from where he stands.

The Academy is the primary organizational expression of the Repentant in the current story.

## Key Distinctions

The three most commonly confused categories:

| Term | Origin | Status | Relationship to Fallen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fallen** | Original divine court | Alive, ancient, embodied | Are themselves |
| **Nephilim (original)** | Fallen + human union | Dead (killed in the flood) | Children of Fallen |
| **Nephilim (operational usage)** | Nephilim-descended lineages | Living biological beings | Grandchildren of Fallen and beyond |
| **Demons** | Dead Nephilim spirits | Disembodied, possessing | Children of Fallen — persisting without bodies |

The Fallen are the source. The original Nephilim carried that inheritance through human biology and are now dead — their spirits persist as demons. Their biological lineages persist as living Nephilim-descended beings.

A demon was once a living being with Nephilim heritage. A living "Nephilim" is a descendant of the original generation, not one of them. A Fallen is neither — older than both, still embodied, still operational.

## The Organization and Its Structures

### Solomon's Organization
The secret society descended from Solomon's court that executes the mandate. No canonical name established yet. Referred to internally and in project files as "the organization." Structured around the twelve tribes. Has operated continuously for approximately three thousand years.

### The Twelve Tribes
The organizational structure. Each tribe has a distinct function developed over three thousand years of operational necessity. Tribal identity is cultural, not ethnic — members are not necessarily of any particular heritage, and family lines are continuity of upbringing rather than bloodline requirements. See individual tribe files.

### The Council
All twelve tribes hold seats. Solomon holds absolute authority when present. Ephraim presides during Solomon's absent periods. Governance structure is designed for continuity — Ephraim administers permanently, not only during absences, so that nothing changes when Solomon dies except the absence of his direct guidance above the administrative layer.

### The Anti-Council
The Fallen's organizational structure, mirroring Solomon's twelve tribes. Operates on pure self-interest rather than covenant mandate. Internal conflict is frequent. Key members: Ashtoreth, Moloch, Chemosh. Not fully mapped — the mirroring structure is known; the full membership is not.

### The Academy
An informal association of Repentant Fallen operating from the decommissioned naval hospital complex on Seavey Island in Portsmouth Harbor, New Hampshire. Not officially sanctioned, not officially unsanctioned. Functionally a mutual accountability support group organized around supernatural research. The Dean is its central figure. See the Academy section of the DIALOGUE-PAGE.md for full detail — a dedicated Academy file is pending.

## Theological and Covenant Terms

### The Covenant
The working agreement between God and the Abrahamic lineage, in its pre-denominational form. The organization holds the covenant in the form it existed before Judaism, Christianity, and Islam each developed their distinctive elaborations. Not hostile to any of the three traditions — simply prior to them. See [covenant.md](covenant.md) and [covenant-abrahamic.md](covenant-abrahamic.md).

### Hesed
Hebrew term for covenant faithfulness — not mere rule compliance but the quality of loyal relationship that honors the covenant bond. The standard by which the organization measures faithfulness. God's hesed is why the covenant persists through failure. The organization's hesed is what faithfulness actually means in practice.

### The Ornias Doctrine
The foundational cautionary principle drawn from Solomon's original fall: *"Beware any solution that arrives exactly when desperation peaks. It may be confirmation that you have stopped trusting what you were already given — completely given, not partially."* Taught by Levi and Ephraim together. Applies institutionally and personally. See [covenant.md](covenant.md).

### Solomonic Ground
Locations consecrated by Levi for organizational use — safe houses, meeting locations, ritual sites. Have anomalous threshold profiles that exceed what the sulfur framework fully accounts for. Protected in a way that is partly chemical and partly something else.

## Operational Terms

### The Sulfur Framework
The organization's current best operational model for supernatural biology. Explains approximately 75% of observed supernatural phenomena through sulfur threshold chemistry. Reliable for field doctrine and protocol. Incomplete — the 25% it cannot account for suggests a deeper unified theory, likely metaphysical rather than chemical. See [sulfur-framework.md](sulfur-framework.md).

### The Three Thresholds
The sulfur framework's core mechanism: First (toxicity — normal beings die here), Second (metaphysical connection — supernatural capability originates here), Third (forced transfer — biological death but metaphysical persistence). See [sulfur-framework.md](sulfur-framework.md).

### Solomonic Seals
The binding knowledge maintained by Levi, originally delivered through the Ornias incident by a Fallen entity posing as the Archangel Michael. Functional. Fallen-sourced. Used against demons, Nephilim threats, and for site consecration. The parental authority seals are a specialized class that exploit the Fallen/Demon lineage relationship. See [levi.md](tribes/levi.md).

### The Martyrs
Willing vessel candidates who complete the soul transfer to Solomon. Venerated within the organization. Solomon insists on this without exception. Their names are held in Manasseh's archive as sacred record. The only acknowledgment they will ever receive. See [issachar.md](tribes/issachar.md) and [tribes-general.md](tribes-general.md).

### The Vessel Pipeline
Issachar's ongoing program of identifying, evaluating, and vetting external candidates for Solomon's soul transfer. Runs continuously — not activated by Solomon's death but maintained permanently in preparation for it. See [issachar.md](tribes/issachar.md).

### The Absent Period
The gap between Solomon's death and his return to operational status. Entirely dependent on Issachar's pipeline readiness. Has ranged from days to decades. The organization's most vulnerable window. See [solomon.md](solomon.md) and [issachar.md](tribes/issachar.md).
