## Council Structure
- All twelve tribes hold council seats
- Solomon holds absolute authority when present
- Ephraim presides during Solomon's absent years — contested by Benjamin
- Ephraim/Benjamin fault line is the central governance tension

---

## Membership
- Not strictly bloodline — adoption close to birth, raised entirely within the society
- Each tribe has its own criteria for identifying and selecting infants
- Tribal identity is cultural, not ethnic — members are not necessarily Jewish
- Leaving is profoundly disorienting — members know too much and belong nowhere outside
