## Foundational Doctrine
Ephraim co-teaches with Levi the organizational cautionary doctrine drawn from the Ornias incident:
*"Beware any solution that arrives exactly when desperation peaks. It may be confirmation that you have stopped trusting what you were already given — completely given, not partially."*

For Ephraim this has a governance dimension — they preside during the organization's most vulnerable periods. A council under pressure is a council susceptible to exactly the kind of shortcut that compromised Solomon. The specific error to watch for is not impatience or bad faith but the quiet belief that this situation is different — that the covenant's prior provision doesn't quite cover what they're now facing. That belief is what the Fallen induced in Solomon. Part of Ephraim's role is recognizing and naming that state before it produces a decision.
