## The Archive
- Three thousand years of organizational history
- The martyr record — the names, lives, and circumstances of every willing host — is among the archive's most sacred holdings. Solomon insists on this. Manasseh maintains it as venerated record, not merely institutional history. Every name is in there. None are redacted.
- The documented death cycles are among the archive's most studied holdings — case files on the Late Bronze Age Collapse, the fall of Rome, the Mongol/Black Death period, the Thirty Years War, and WWI/WWII. These are the worst and most unmistakable; the full record contains others.
- What is kept beyond the major cycles: *to be developed*
- What has been lost, destroyed, or suppressed: *to be developed* — the Bronze Age records are known to be fragmentary; what else is missing and why is an open question
