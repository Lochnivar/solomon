## Foundational Doctrine
The Ornias incident is central to Levi's institutional culture. Their knowledge base is Fallen-sourced — delivered via a being impersonating Michael during Solomon's moment of desperation at the Temple. Levi knows this. The engineering conservatism is partly a response to it: they maintain the seals because they work, and they work because of what they actually are. Devotion would require trusting the source. They don't.

Levi teaches the operational doctrine shared with Ephraim:
*"Beware any solution that arrives exactly when desperation peaks. It may be confirmation that you have stopped trusting what you were already given — completely given, not partially."*

The doctrine's theological foundation: an omniscient God's provision is total, not situational. The Gibeon gift was not wisdom for ordinary governance. It was wisdom for everything. The belief that a new crisis exceeds prior provision is the first error. It does not require conscious unbelief — only the feeling that this situation is different. That feeling is the opening.
