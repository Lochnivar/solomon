# Monster Lineages — Genus and Family Reference

Working document. First pass only — assignments marked confirmed, proposed, or TBD. Expand as new lineages are identified and mapped.

---

## Framework

Every monster lineage traces back to a Fallen progenitor. The Fallen's domain — their corrupted expression of whatever divine nature they originally carried — shapes what kind of supernatural being their Nephilim descendants became. That nature passed through human biology, was destabilized and amplified by it, and expressed differently in each lineage depending on the specific Fallen involved.

The three-tier structure:
1. **Fallen progenitor** — the domain origin, the ultimate source of the lineage's nature
2. **Named Demon** — a significant dead Nephilim of that bloodline, the proximate ancestor (to be developed per lineage)
3. **Living lineage** — the modern monster family, still expressing the Fallen's domain through their biology

Gad's lineage-specific doctrine is essentially this framework applied operationally: each family's vulnerabilities, behavioral patterns, and known weaknesses reflect their Fallen progenitor's nature. Levi's parental authority seals work against specific lineages precisely because they invoke the Fallen progenitor's authority over their bloodline.

## Confirmed Assignments

### Lilith → Vampiric Lineages
**Domain:** Blood predation, night, the liminal space between life and death.

The vampiric lineages — bloodline vampires, strigoi, various regional variants — trace to Lilith. Her Nephilim children carried a specific relationship with blood as the medium of life, which expressed through the forced third-threshold transfer mechanism: biological death interrupted by metaphysical connection sustained through blood feeding. The blood dependency isn't incidental to vampire biology — it is the Lilith nature expressing through the sulfur framework. Blood was always the covenant medium. Lilith's domain corrupted it.

Regional variants and distinct bloodlines within vampire lineages represent different Nephilim sublines from Lilith's broader family — each somewhat distinct in behavior and capability, unified by the blood-dependency mechanism.

*Operational note: The fire-in-sunlight mythology is Naphtali misdirection. See sulfur-framework.md for actual sunlight mechanism.*

---

### Azazel → Lycanthrope and Were-Lineages
**Domain:** The physical world, animal nature, transformation — Azazel was the Watcher most directly engaged with physical matter, who taught humanity to work with it.

Lycanthropes, werebears, weretigers, and the broader spectrum of animal-transformation lineages trace to Azazel. His Nephilim children inherited a specific relationship with animal physicality — the ability to exist in multiple biological registers — which the sulfur framework expresses through the transformation mechanism: concentration spikes driving rapid physical restructuring. The lunar connection is the tidal-sulfur pathway (see sulfur-framework.md) but the underlying reason that pathway produces *animal* transformation rather than some other kind is the Azazel nature.

Different were-lineages represent different animal expressions of the same core mechanism. Why wolves vs. bears vs. big cats as the specific transformation targets is a Levi open question — the working hypothesis is that the specific animal reflects the Nephilim subline, each having developed an affinity for a particular species over millennia.

*Operational note: Silver remains the primary countermeasure. The sulfidation mechanism works against all Azazel-lineage entities.*

---

### Dagon → Aquatic Lineages
**Domain:** Maritime power, the deep, the ungoverned space beneath the surface.

Deep ones, merfolk, and the broader spectrum of aquatic supernatural entities trace to Dagon. The oldest maritime supernatural presence in the organizational record. Dagon's Nephilim children adapted to aquatic environments in ways that produced lineages that are genuinely at home in the deep ocean in ways no purely human-descended biology could sustain. The sulfur chemistry of aquatic lineages shows distinctive patterns — pressure tolerance, respiratory adaptation, sensory systems calibrated for deep water — that reflect the Dagon domain expressed through threshold biology.

Dagon lineages are among the most politically organized of the monster families, maintaining their own power structures in areas the organization has historically had limited reach. Zebulun's most difficult supernatural diplomatic relationships are in this space.

*Operational note: Gad's aquatic doctrine is the least developed of the major lineages — operational access to deep-water environments remains a standing capability gap.*

---

### Resheph → Plague Lineages
**Domain:** Disease, pestilence, bodily corruption, fever — the body turning against itself.

Ghouls, zombies, pestilence creatures, and related reanimation lineages trace to Resheph. The specific sulfur chemistry of plague lineages runs through decay chemistry rather than the standard organosulfur pathways — the threshold connection in these entities is maintained through biological corruption rather than biological vitality. Where vampires sustain their metaphysical connection through blood feeding (maintaining life chemistry), plague lineages sustain it through ongoing decay processes. They are, in a specific technical sense, dying continuously and continuously not completing the process.

Resheph lineages are the most behaviorally chaotic of the confirmed families — the decay chemistry produces deteriorating cognitive function over time, which means older plague-lineage entities are often less capable than younger ones. This is the inverse of most other lineages. Gad doctrine accounts for this.

*Operational note: Standard organosulfur countermeasures have attenuated effect on plague lineages due to the different threshold chemistry. Levi is developing lineage-specific binding modifications.*

## Proposed Assignments

*Strong candidates — pending confirmation.*

### Semyaza → Giant and Ancient Lineages / The Fae
**Domain:** Leadership of the Watcher descent — the most direct engagement with the Nephilim creation itself.

As the organizer of the Watchers' descent, Semyaza's Nephilim children are the oldest and in some cases most powerful Nephilim lineages. Giants, titans, and the ancient entities whose independent cultures the Fae traditions across global folklore were observing — these trace plausibly to Semyaza. His lineage represents the Nephilim at their most developed, with the most independent history.

*Status: Proposed. The Fae connection requires development — whether the Fae tradition represents a single Semyaza lineage or multiple Fallen lineages is unresolved.*

---

### Ashtoreth → Corruption and Identity Lineages
**Domain:** Corruption, seduction, infiltration — the slow compromise of what something actually is.

Entities whose primary supernatural nature involves corrupting or replacing identity — doppelgangers, changelings, succubi/incubi, shape-shifters whose shifts are about becoming something else rather than becoming an animal (contrast with Azazel's were-lineages). The Ashtoreth domain is the long compromise. Her lineage's expressions involve entities who are something other than what they present as.

*Status: Proposed. The distinction between Ashtoreth identity-shifters and Azazel animal-shifters needs further development.*

---

### Moloch → Consumption and Battlefield Lineages
**Domain:** Sacrifice, mass death, the consumption of suffering as power.

Entities associated with mass death, battlefield carnage, and the extraction of energy from suffering — wendigo, certain wraith lineages, entities that are empowered by violence and death rather than feeding on blood or decay. The Moloch nature expressed through biology produces something that grows stronger in conditions of mass human suffering.

*Status: Proposed. The wendigo assignment in particular needs review — the consumption aspect fits Moloch but the isolation/winter aspects may suggest a different lineage.*

---

### Mammon → Draconic and Hoarder Lineages
**Domain:** Material accumulation, the corruption of provision into possession.

Dragons, treasure-guardian entities, and lineages defined by territorial possession and accumulation. The Mammon nature expressed through Nephilim biology produces entities whose drive toward material accumulation becomes a physical characteristic — the hoard isn't just behavior, it's biology. Some draconic lineages may have the longest organizational history of any monster family, their ancestors having accumulated resources and territory since before the organization existed.

*Status: Proposed. Dragon biology under the sulfur framework requires development — the fire mechanism in particular.*

---

### Nisroch → Shadow and Assassination Lineages
**Domain:** Military intelligence, operating through human power structures, removal of targets.

Shadow entities, assassin-class supernatural beings, entities that operate through human institutional structures. The Nisroch connection (2 Kings 19:37 — Sennacherib killed by his sons in Nisroch's temple) suggests a domain of power exercised through proximity and betrayal. Lineage entities would express this through abilities related to concealment, infiltration of human spaces, and targeted removal.

*Status: Proposed. Least developed of the proposed assignments.*

## TBD — Lineages Without Confirmed Progenitors

**Djinn** — Fire, wind, the Arabian tradition. The specific Islamic theological context makes mapping to our Fallen roster complicated. May require identifying a Fallen specifically from the Mesopotamian or Arabian divine tradition. The sulfur framework connection is unclear — some djinn behavior suggests second-threshold biology, some suggests something different.

**Storm entities** — Thunder beings, storm giants, entities associated with atmospheric phenomena. Baal is a storm deity in the original tradition — this may be a Baal lineage.

**Trickster entities** — Kitsune, Coyote-tradition entities, Loki-adjacent beings across cultures. May be Ashtoreth (identity corruption) or a separate trickster-domain Fallen not yet identified.

**Dream entities** — Night terrors, entities that operate in sleep states. Lilith has some traditional association with this (the lilim/night-terror tradition) but this may be a separate Fallen domain.

**Spectral lineages** — Ghosts, poltergeists, hauntings. The theological question: are these demons (dead Nephilim) or are they something else entirely? The organizational position is unsettled.

**Cryptid edge cases** — Bigfoot/Sasquatch, Loch Ness, Chupacabra, and the broader folkloric cryptid roster. Whether these are distinct Nephilim lineages, regional variants of established families, or genuinely mundane animals misidentified is assessed case by case. Gad maintains case files. Dan maps the distribution. Some are confirmed lineages. Some are Naphtali-managed folklore. A few remain genuinely unclassified.
